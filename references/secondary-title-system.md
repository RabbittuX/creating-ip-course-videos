# Secondary-title B-roll

The source script's heading structure is an input, not narration. Before extracting TTS text, save a `planning/heading-ledger.json` containing the source-script path/hash and, for every `##` heading, its chapter, exact text, source line, order, classification (`audience-facing` or `editorial`), and the first spoken paragraph it introduces. An unnumbered heading such as `## 开头` or `## 结尾` is not automatically excluded: mark it editorial only when it is a script organizer rather than an intended on-screen section title, and record the reason. Never infer this classification from TTS output, which has already discarded the headings.

Every audience-facing second-level heading produces exactly one non-narrated B-roll title card immediately before that section's first spoken line. Use the single fixed `assets/broll/fixed/subchapter.html` template. Preserve its layout, typography, color, motion, sound design, and character assets; change only its designated title/number/chapter fields and hold duration. Do not substitute a functional B-roll or generate an alternate layout. The title card is a separator: it does not carry narration, captions, or narration character spans.

Insert the title hold into the master timeline, shifting the following narration audio, captions, A-roll, B-roll, effects, and subsequent title cards by the same duration. End or cut the preceding visual at the title boundary; do not stack the title over another active B-roll. After the hold, resume at the section's first spoken word. A title may have its own fixed cue sound if the template provides one.

Before first preview, compare the heading ledger with the timeline and reject the build unless the audience-facing heading count, exact text, chapter order, anchor order, and one-to-one fixed-template composition IDs match. Also reject any title interval with narration/caption playback or visual overlap, and any spoken interval omitted or duplicated by the time shift. Re-run the comparison after script, title timing, or speed edits; a previous preview approval does not cover a changed heading ledger.
