# Douyin content preflight for narrated course videos

Use this review at the start of a project, before the source 口播稿 is indexed. Its basis is Douyin/巨量星图's public “内容营销规范升级公告” (dated 2025-12-24) and its guidance on viewer-direction phrases and stickers. The notice links to [抖音社区自律公约](https://www.douyin.com/rule/policy#heading-13). Platform rules can change; check the current official requirements before publication. This is an editorial risk review, not a guarantee of platform approval.

## Narration check

Read the whole script, including the opening, quoted prompts that will be spoken, outro, and calls to action. Look for language that tells viewers to leave Douyin for a third-party app, site, shop, private contact, or unclear transaction channel to download, use, buy, or claim a reward. The screenshots specifically call out constructions such as “点击/上/找/打开/去/试试/推荐用/下载/搜索 + 某 App or destination.” Treat the action and destination together: “打开素材文件” is different from “打开某 App”; a neutral description of a tool used in the creator's own workflow is not automatically a viewer instruction.

Also flag viewer-facing promises of free trials, prizes, benefits, or access when paired with a download/use direction; and oral instructions to follow an arrow, pointing hand, QR code, profile, link, or private message toward an external destination. Do not assume a name-only mention is a violation. Do not advise disguised wording that preserves the same off-platform direction.

For each finding, give the exact excerpt and location, explain the viewer action it implies, and propose the smallest rewrite that keeps the teaching point while removing that direction. Prefer demonstrations and factual feature descriptions, such as showing the operation performed in the video, over telling viewers to install or use an outside product. When a phrase is ambiguous, mark it for editorial judgment rather than treating a keyword match as proof. If the source is locked, request the creator's revised or approved wording before indexing; do not silently change canonical narration.

## Visual-outline check

At visual-outline review, repeat the audit for title cards, subtitles, prompt cards, UI mockups, end cards, and other viewer-facing text. Flag arrows, pointing-hand stickers, cursor callouts, QR codes, download badges, and similar elements when they direct attention to an external app, profile, link, or transaction channel. A normal cursor used to demonstrate an on-screen editing step is not automatically a diversion cue; judge its destination and context. Replace risky cues with a plain depiction of the workflow or feature, then review the revised screen.

## Report format

State `PASS` or `FINDINGS`. For each finding include `位置｜原文或画面｜风险原因｜最小修改建议｜待确认事项`. A pass means no issue found in the supplied materials at that stage, not that the final upload is approved. Re-run the relevant check whenever narration or audience-facing visuals change.
